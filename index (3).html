Our farm in Gir

Mitti Organic is based in Umrathi, near Talala in Gir Somnath, the home of the Gir cow. We make ghee and collect honey in small batches, and send it from here to your home.

Questions we get asked

How do I order?

Add products to the cart, enter your name, phone and address, then press Order on WhatsApp. Your order opens in WhatsApp. Press Send and we will confirm it.

Which areas do you deliver to?

We deliver across Gujarat. We confirm the delivery charge and time on WhatsApp when we receive your order.

How do I pay?

You can pay by cash on delivery (COD) or by UPI. For UPI, pick UPI in the cart, scan the QR code or pay to jenilvadhiya78@okicici, then send the payment screenshot on WhatsApp with your order.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Message us on WhatsApp the same day with a photo of the parcel and the product, and we will sort it out.

Can I visit the farm?

Yes, by appointment. Call or WhatsApp +91 96013 87764 to fix a time.
